Agentic Ai:
    agentic ai is an ai system that can observe, make decision, and take actions
    autonomously to achieve a goal.

    examples:
        1. customer support agents: answer customer queries, resolve order issues(like rufus in amazon)
        2. self driving cars: observe traffc obstacles, make decision and control cars
        3. ai assitant: process mail, play songs, set remainders or fullfill any other user request
